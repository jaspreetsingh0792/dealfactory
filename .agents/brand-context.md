# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: DealFactory
- **Category**: Personal AI Shopping Memory & Compatibility Advisor for Smart Home and Home Appliances
- **Description**: DealFactory remembers the smart-home devices and home appliances you already own — from Gmail receipts or manual entry — and checks every new purchase for compatibility and live retailer prices.
- **Stage**: Early / Launch
- **Website**: https://dealfactory-3uu.pages.dev/ (planned custom domain: getdealfactory.com)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Tech-curious European & Dutch homeowners and renters (25–45) who own a mix of smart-home devices and home appliances (Philips Hue, Apple Home, Google Home, Home Assistant, LG/Samsung TVs, robot vacuums, air purifiers, espresso machines, boilers/thermostats) and hate buying accessories or parts that don't work with their setup.
- **Key Problem**: Smart-home protocols (Matter, Zigbee, Thread, OpenTherm) and appliance accessories/filters are fragmented and confusing. General AI chats hallucinate compatibility and quote stale or US-only prices; merchant-funded apps push sponsored products instead of honest fit.
- **Their Language**: "Does this work with Apple Home / Home Assistant?", "Matter over Thread vs Zigbee", "OpenTherm compatible?", "Will this fit my exact model?", "Which filter fits my Philips purifier?", "No hub required?", "Actual price in NL/EU", "Don't sell my data".

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Buyer-side, store-independent, privacy-first shopping memory. Starts from what you *already own* (imported from Gmail receipts or entered manually), checks exact compatibility first, verifies live retailer prices via `/mcp`, and exports as an open `Shopping Context` JSON usable in Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP tool.
- **Competitors**: Google Gemini + Google Shopping (ad-funded, locked to Google), Shopify Shop app (merchant-funded, Shopify stores only), Klarna (payments/deals focus), ChatGPT & Perplexity Shopping (no structured owned-device memory, frequent EU price/fit hallucinations).
- **Market Position**: Niche, independent, high-trust utility — zero ads, free and open source, bring-your-own-AI.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Grounded, Exact, Independent, Calm, Candid
- **Tone**: Casual-expert and serious-helpful (never salesy, never corporate, never hyped)
- **Voice Admires**: Wirecutter (honest testing), Tweakers (Dutch tech-spec rigor), Linear / iFixit (calm, buyer-first clarity)

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Fit before price, Buyer-side independence, Radical privacy (browser-local, zero ads), Verifiable truth (no invented specs or prices), Open & portable data
- **Mission**: Give every household a private, portable memory of what they own so they never waste money on a smart-home device, cable, or appliance part that doesn't fit.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Build organic search traffic ("footfall") and repeat usage around high-intent Smart Home & Home Appliance compatibility guides and the personal advisor + MCP connector.
- **Key Metrics**: Organic visits to compatibility guides, Shopping Context setups created, Gmail/manual imports, Claude/ChatGPT MCP connections, verified retailer outbound clicks.
