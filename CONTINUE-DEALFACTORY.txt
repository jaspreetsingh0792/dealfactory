## Gmail import progress

Published from `5c9b0095a450ba88ecf81233e4b22defda67ec55` (#26):
https://github.com/jaspreetsingh0792/dealfactory/actions/runs/45945467317

Live `/advisor/` loads `/app.js?v=4304bac836ba` (`Cache-Control: no-store`). Step 2 has a polite progress line and a determinate bar in Forest Pine, Warm Paper, and Chart Lime. The phases are signing in, searching receipts, reading message X of N, extracting with the selected provider, and a done line that links to Your setup. Cancel aborts the request and keeps items already extracted. A 429 or 5xx says Gmail is busy and names the retry delay. A failed message is counted as skipped. An empty search names the selected range. The Connect button stays disabled while the import runs. Progress text is counts only. `gmail_import_start`, `gmail_import`, and `gmail_import_cancel` are counted without message contents.
