PRIVACY

Shopping advisor privacy.

Your shopping setup

Your browser stores the preferences, purchases and results you save. Starting a search sends your request and shareable shopping setup to the AI provider you select. Opening a brief in Claude or ChatGPT sends that brief to the service you open.

Gmail receipt import

If you choose to connect Gmail, DealFactory requests read-only Gmail access. Your browser reads your profile address for redaction, searches for likely receipts and order confirmations, and retrieves matching messages. It filters and redacts the text before sending excerpts to your selected AI provider to extract purchased items. Redaction can miss personal information. The provider receives those excerpts under its own privacy and retention terms.

The advisor saves extracted purchase details, deduplication references and your confirmations in this browser. DealFactory's hosted server receives neither the Gmail access token nor the receipt text. Gmail excerpts are sent to the AI provider you select only to provide the user-facing import (identifying purchased items for your setup), never for advertising. DealFactory does not use Google user data to train AI models.

DealFactory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Removing data and access

Use Delete all imported purchases in the Gmail section to remove the import history. Items you confirmed separately remain in your setup. Use Delete everything stored here in the advisor's About section to clear the advisor on this device. You can revoke Gmail access in your Google account connections. To revoke an OpenRouter key, use your OpenRouter key settings.

Deal checks and traffic

The public shopping connector receives the retailer URL and any price or quote you ask it to check. The storefront can count page views, shares and retailer clicks in aggregate. The receipt import runs separately from those counters.
