PRIVACY

Shopping advisor privacy.

Your shopping setup

Your browser stores the preferences, purchases and results you save. Starting a search sends your request and shareable shopping setup to the built-in AI, or to the AI provider you select if you choose your own. Opening a brief in Claude or ChatGPT sends that brief to the service you open.

Accounts

Signing in with Google or GitHub is optional. The sign-in asks only for an identity (OpenID, email and profile for Google; read-only user and email for GitHub). It does not ask for Gmail. DealFactory stores an account id, the provider, the provider's account id, your email, a display name and the time the account was created. A session cookie (HttpOnly) remembers the sign-in in this browser. Daily counts of built-in AI requests are stored so the free allowance can be enforced. Prompts are not stored.

Sign out ends the session. Delete account removes the account, its sessions and its usage counts from DealFactory. Shopping setup saved in this browser is separate; use Delete everything stored here to clear it.

Built-in AI

After you sign in, the advisor can answer with Cloudflare Workers AI, using the Llama 3.1 8B instruct model hosted by Cloudflare. The browser and the server remove recognizable personal details before a prompt is sent. DealFactory does not log the prompt. Cloudflare processes the redacted text to produce the answer. There is a per-person daily allowance and a site-wide daily ceiling so the service stays on the free allocation. You can ignore the built-in AI and connect your own provider instead. That provider then receives the request under its own terms.

Gmail receipt import

If you choose to connect Gmail, DealFactory requests read-only Gmail access. Your browser reads your profile address for redaction, searches for likely receipts and order confirmations, and retrieves matching messages. It filters and redacts the text before sending excerpts to whichever AI provider you pick in the advisor: OpenRouter, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Groq, an OpenAI-compatible address you enter, or Ollama on your computer. Redaction can miss personal information. That provider receives those excerpts under its own privacy and retention terms. The API key stays in this browser, and the call goes from the browser to the provider.

The advisor saves extracted purchase details, deduplication references and your confirmations in this browser. DealFactory's hosted server receives neither the Gmail access token nor the receipt text. Gmail excerpts are sent only to the built-in AI or to the AI provider you pick, and only to identify purchased items for your setup, never for advertising. DealFactory does not use Google user data to train AI models.

DealFactory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Removing data and access

Use Delete all imported purchases in the Gmail section to remove the import history. Items you confirmed separately remain in your setup. Use Delete everything stored here in the advisor's About section to clear the advisor on this device. You can revoke Gmail access in your Google account connections. To revoke an OpenRouter key, use your OpenRouter key settings.

Deal checks and traffic

The public shopping connector receives the retailer URL and any price or quote you ask it to check. The storefront can count page views, shares and retailer clicks in aggregate. The receipt import runs separately from those counters.
