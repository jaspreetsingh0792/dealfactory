      Your AI

      Your key is sent only to the provider you chose, straight from this browser. There is no DealFactory server. You pay your provider for the searches; one need is usually a few cents to a few tens of cents.

      Use it with your own assistant

      Your setup is saved as a Shopping Context, an open JSON format any assistant or tool can read. Move it between this site, ChatGPT, Claude and anything else.

      1 · Give your context to a chat

      Copy a ready-made prompt with your context and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. They'll use it for shopping questions.

      2 · Let your assistant fill it in

      Your assistant may already know what you own. Copy this request, ask it, then paste its reply below. Everything comes in as unconfirmed.
