      Use it with your own assistant

      Your setup is saved as a Shopping Context, an open JSON format any assistant or tool can read. Move it between this site, ChatGPT, Claude and anything else.

      Copy a brief into your preferred assistant or download your setup for another tool.

      Your assistant may already know what you own. Copy this request, ask it, then paste its reply below. Everything comes in as unconfirmed.
